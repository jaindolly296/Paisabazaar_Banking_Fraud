# 🏦 PaisaBazaar Banking Fraud & Credit Risk Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer financial and loan application data from PaisaBazaar to identify fraud indicators, evaluate customer creditworthiness, and discover factors affecting loan approval and repayment behavior.

Using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, the project performs comprehensive data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and business insight generation to support better credit risk management.

---

# Business Objective

Financial institutions receive thousands of loan applications daily. Detecting high-risk applicants early helps reduce defaults and financial losses.

This project aims to:

- Identify customers with high fraud risk.
- Analyze factors influencing loan approval.
- Improve credit risk assessment.
- Support data-driven lending decisions.
- Reduce loan default rates.

---

# Problem Statement

The objective is to analyze customer demographic, financial, and credit-related information to determine the key factors associated with fraudulent behavior and loan repayment risk.

The analysis focuses on:

- Credit score impact
- Income stability
- Loan amount
- Employment type
- Credit utilization
- Repayment history
- Customer risk segmentation

# Key Business Insights

- Customers with lower credit scores have higher default probability.
- High credit utilization is strongly associated with repayment risk.
- Larger loans and longer tenure increase financial risk.
- Stable income improves repayment behavior.
- Certain customer segments require additional verification before loan approval.
